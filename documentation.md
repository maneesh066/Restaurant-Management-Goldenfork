# 🍴 Goldenfork - Restaurant Management System Documentation

## 1. Introduction
**Goldenfork** is a state-of-the-art, premium restaurant management and customer ordering platform. It is designed to bridge the gap between traditional dining and digital efficiency, providing a seamless experience for customers, kitchen staff, and restaurant managers.

The system focuses on high-end aesthetics (Golden/Dark theme), real-time operational transparency, and robust customer engagement through feedback and complaint management.

---

## 2. Core Features
### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Customer Features
*   **Digital Menu**: Interactive, category-based browsing (Breakfast, Lunch, Combo Sections, etc.).
*   **Smart Cart**: Manual quantity input and real-time total calculation with GST.
*   **Order Tracking**: Real-time status updates (Pending -> Cooking -> Ready).
*   **Dual Payment Options**: Support for "Pay at Counter" (Cash) and "UPI" with screenshot verification.
*   **Interactive Notifications**: Instant alerts when food preparation begins.

### 👨‍🍳 Kitchen Operations
*   **Dynamic Order Queue**: Categorized view of active orders.
*   **Stock Management**: Real-time inventory tracking with manual override capability.
*   **Customer Interaction**: Ability to receive "Alerts" from customers and send custom replies regarding preparation delays.

### ⚙️ Managerial Controls
*   **Sales Analytics**: Filterable sales reports (Daily, Monthly, Yearly, or Custom Date).
*   **Menu CRUD**: Full control over menu items, categories, and availability.
*   **QR Management**: Centralized hub for generating and viewing Table Ordering QRs and Review QRs.
*   **Complaint Resolution**: Dedicated panel to verify, reply to, and resolve customer grievances.

---

## 3. Recent Enhancements (Project Refinements)
*   **Automated Email/OTP System**: Integrated Gmail SMTP for secure complaint registration and status tracking.
*   **Review & Rating Hub**: 5-star rating system accessible via unique Review QRs.
*   **Order Cancellation**: Graceful cancellation logic for customers before kitchen confirmation.
*   **Enhanced Validation**: Strict 10-digit phone and properly formatted email validation.
*   **UI/UX Polish**: Smooth scrolling navigation, micro-animations, and a unified golden-dark premium theme.
